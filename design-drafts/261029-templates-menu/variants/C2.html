How it works

  • Chips: All · ★ Saved · No camera · One camera · Two cameras. Camera chips filter your saved templates too. The chip you pick is remembered for the next clip.
  • Order in All: “＋ Save this edit”, your saved templates (last used first, ★ tag), a thin “Eklipse templates” divider, then the 11 presets.
  • One way to apply. Now that Eklipse templates also carry captions + sticker, both kinds apply a full look, so mixing them in one grid no longer confuses. That was C’s main con. Saved cards list everything they bring; presets show their caption text on the thumbnail.
  • Whole clip. On a split clip a banner says so before you pick; the toast says “both parts”, and Undo restores the per-part layouts. A 16:9 clip becomes 9:16.
  • Manage in place. ⋯ on a saved card: Rename (inline, Enter saves, Esc cancels) and Delete (toast with Undo). Presets have no ⋯.
  • No templates yet. All opens with a “Make your own template” card above the Eklipse templates: what a template keeps, one button to save this edit, and a pointer to start from an Eklipse template. ★ Saved shows 0 and the same card, with “Browse Eklipse templates”. A remembered ★ Saved chip falls back to All while there is nothing saved. After the first save the card is gone and the new template sits first, name field open.

  • Caption style + sticker pairing per Eklipse template (drafted from the Captions catalog; product to confirm).
  • Saved template applies what it was saved with (sounds, effects too); Eklipse templates keep your text, effects and sounds.
