B3 · Hover a look to add; the video is for move and remove

Same panel as B2, but no "+ Add sticker" mode. You add from wherever your mouse already is:

1 · Hover a lookA slim "+ Add" appears on the tile and a live preview shows on the video. Click to add.
2 · Add only from the panelThe video has no add button; it is for checking, moving and removing.
3 · Remove on the videoHover or select a sticker: ✕ in its corner, or press Delete.
4 · Move itDrag any sticker on the video. Eklipse designs are cut slim, like StreamLadder's.

What changed from B2

  • No add mode. The grid always adds; B2's "+ Add sticker" then pick is gone.
  • Hover a look: only "+ Add", plus a live preview on the video. To change a sticker's look: remove it and add the new one.
  • On the video: click to select, drag to move, ✕ or Delete to remove. No adding on the video.
  • Remove from the sticker card ✕, the video ✕, Delete, or "Remove" in the sheet.

Kept from B2

  • One name per sticker. Multi looks show several platform icons with that one name. A different name on another platform = another sticker.
  • Up to 3 stickers; When = whole clip, first 5 s, last 5 s.
  • Tint every look; "Use these stickers on every new clip"; last platform remembered.
  • New stickers keep the colour shown in the grid.

Edge cases in the mock

  • 3 stickers: "+ Add" on tiles disabled with a reason.
  • First time: adding asks for the name in the sheet.
  • Typing a name: Delete/Backspace edits the text, it does not remove the sticker.
  • Touch / keyboard: tiles are focusable; "+ Add" shows on focus, so no hover is needed.
  • 16:9 clip: same, stickers stay left-aligned.
