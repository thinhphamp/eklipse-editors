A · Compact chip group smallest change

Same place, half the weight: one 28 px group, icon + platform name, real zoom value.

How it works

  • Safe zone: icon + “TikTok” (Label 12). Menu: No safe zones · YouTube · TikTok · Instagram.
  • Zoom: “Fit · 66%” → menu with Fit to window, 200 … 25 %.
  • Top gap 96 → 52 px; Fit uses ~88 % of the free height so the player gets air above and below.

Pros

  • Tiny code change (CSS + label), nothing moves for returning users.
  • Matches the top bar's secondary buttons.

Cons

  • Still far from the player; still a separate pattern from the Templates tab.

Edge cases

  • 16:9 output: safe-zone chip hidden, zoom stays.
  • Panel collapsed: expand button stays left in the same 28 px row.
  • Templates tab: the same group sits under the 9:16 preview instead of the full-width select.
  • Booting (?start=app): group at 45 % opacity, not clickable.

B · Toolbar under the player recommended

The controls belong to the preview, so they live with it: one small floating bar centred under the phone.

How it works

  • [safe-zone icon · TikTok ⌄] | [− Fit · 66% +]. − / + step through the zoom list, the value opens it.
  • Top-right corner is empty; stage top gap 96 → 36 px; player 400 px, bar below it.
  • Same bar in every tab, also under the 9:16 preview on Templates (replaces the full-width select).
  • Shortcuts ⌘− / ⌘= stay on the timeline zoom; preview zoom gets the buttons only.

Pros

  • Reads as “settings of this preview”; one pattern everywhere.
  • Direct − / + is faster than a 10-item list.
  • Lightest top of the stage: player is the only thing up there.

Cons

  • Bar moves with the player height (window/timeline resize) — needs the stage-fit math to reserve 42 px below.
  • Close to the playback bar; must not look like a second player bar.

Edge cases

  • Zoom > 100 %: bar pins to the stage bottom, does not scroll away with the phone.
  • 16:9 output: only [− value +].
  • Short window: bar keeps 30 px; the player shrinks first.
  • Booting: bar disabled with the rest of the stage.

C · Icon strip beside the player

Smallest footprint: a vertical strip of icon buttons hugging the phone's right edge, like canvas tools.

How it works

  • Safe-zone icon (dot when on) → platform menu. +, %, −, Fit below.
  • A quiet tag above the phone names the active safe zone (“TikTok safe zone”).
  • Tooltips carry the words; no text in the strip.

Pros

  • Almost no visual weight; player gets the most height.
  • Close to the player, always the same spot.

Cons

  • Icon-only: newcomers may not find safe zones (worth a usability probe).
  • Competes with on-canvas element toolbars and the channel-sticker ghost on the right side.

Edge cases

  • Templates tab: strip sits between source and preview — tight; would move under the preview there.
  • Zoom > 100 %: strip pins to the stage's right edge.
  • 16:9 output: safe-zone icon hidden, tag hidden.
