Design draft · captions-tab · round 2 · variant D · 30 Oct 2026

Prototype tabs, new setup, presets of style you can edit

The Text · Effects · Presets tabs stay as in the prototype. What changes: (1) the screen before captions are generated (language, how captions show, auto emoji, preset of style, like production), (2) how the text shows: Word by word or Full phrases on the video, and two ways to list the text in the Text tab (D1 / D2, switch above the mock), and (3) editing a preset of style is back: font, weight, colours, stroke, highlight, saved as your own preset of style.

What changed from the prototype

  • Before generating: no globe-only entry. The Captions tab opens on setup: Caption language, Show captions as Word by word / Full phrases, Auto emoji, Preset of style (4 + "See all"), Generate. The clip shows a live sample.
  • Effects: "Show captions as" sits on top so it can change after generating; Emojis gets its right description. AI Shake, AI Supersize and Random rotation are removed; Hide Punctuation stays.
  • Presets: "My presets of style" first, then the prototype groups. The selected tile has Edit; Edit opens a popup (controls left, big live preview right) with font, weight, italic, AA, size, fill, stroke, highlight. Apply to this clip or Save as my preset of style.
  • Text tab D1 · Lines: one row per line with time range, like production; click to fix, + Add line.
  • Text tab D2 · As on video: one card per caption exactly as it shows on the video. In Word by word, each word is its own chip with its time; click a word to fix it.

Remembered

  • Format, emoji and preset of style carry to the next clips (local storage + saved templates). Try "Returning user".

Edge cases in the mock

  • No speech, split clip (Part 1 / Part 2), 16:9, regenerate after edits (asks first), language changed after generating, editing a built-in preset (saves as a copy; the built-in one stays).
