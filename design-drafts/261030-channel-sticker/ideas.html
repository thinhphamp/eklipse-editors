Design draft · 30 Oct 2026 · round 1 · feature: channel sticker

Channel sticker gets its own tab, 2nd below Templates

Today the sticker lives at the top of Elements as the "brand card". The main Eklipse app has a dedicated Channel Stickers menu with a name, one platform, 7 colours and Single / Multi designs. This board merges both into one tab, then shows three ways to lay it out.

Every mock is clickable: type a name, toggle platforms, pick colours and designs. The chips above each mock switch edge cases.

Round 1 recommendation

A · Set up your channel, then pick a design

The main app's order (name, platforms, colour, then designs) on top of the prototype's saved sticker.

How it works

  • Top card: name, platform toggles (several allowed), colour.
  • The grid below only shows designs that fit your platforms. One platform: single designs. Two or more: multi designs. No tabs to learn.
  • Status row under the card: on this clip, remove, add to every new clip.
  • Grid groups: Eklipse (follow your colour) and More looks (fixed colours, animated PRO).

Pros

  • Same order as the main app: lowest relearning for current users.
  • Grid is always relevant: never shows a Kick design to a Twitch-only streamer.
  • Single/Multi decided for you.

Cons

  • Setup card takes ~230px before the first design.
  • Browsing looks for another platform means toggling platforms.

Edge cases

  • First time: name prefilled from the connected account; with none, tiles show "your name" and a hint.
  • No platform: name-only designs (no icon).
  • 5+ platforms: capped at 4 with a hint.
  • Long name: text shrinks, 24-character cap.
  • Fixed-colour look: swatches grey out with "This look has its own colours".
  • 16:9 clip / split clip: bottom-left on 16:9; whole clip across all parts.

B · Pick a look, then make it yours

The gallery is the panel. Every look is shown once, already with your name and platforms; settings open in a sheet after you pick.

How it works

  • Only the name sits above the grid. Filters: All, Static, Animated, Multi.
  • The catalog repeats the same look per platform (neon, sparkle, pet bars exist for Twitch, YouTube and Facebook). B shows each look once and draws your platform into it: 52 tiles become ~15.
  • Picking a look opens Your sticker at the bottom: platforms, colour, on clip, every new clip.

Pros

  • First design visible right away; best for "show me what's there".
  • Much shorter gallery, no duplicate looks.

Cons

  • Platforms and colour hide in the sheet; main-app users look for them on top.
  • Looks made for one platform (lavender = Twitch) need a "switches to Twitch" note.

Edge cases

  • First time: sheet opens with the name field focused.
  • Look for another platform: tag on the tile, and picking it switches your platforms with an undo.
  • Removed from clip: sheet button reads "Add to this clip".

Alternative · if Multi means different names

C · Your channels, one handle each

A list of your channels, each with its own handle. The sticker shows them all at once, or one at a time.

How it works

  • One row per channel: platform, handle, show on sticker. "+ Add" for the other platforms.
  • All at once uses multi designs; One at a time rotates every 3 s through the channels, in any single design.
  • 6 suggested looks for your mix in a row; "All looks" opens the full gallery page.

Pros

  • Fits streamers whose handle differs per platform (common for YouTube @handles).
  • Rotation gives multi-channel without a crowded sticker.

Cons

  • Most setup of the three; heavier for one-platform streamers.
  • New concept (rotation) to design and engineer; animated export cost.

Edge cases

  • First time: one row prefilled from the connected account.
  • All rows hidden: sticker off, hint to turn one on.
  • Short clip + rotate: clips under 6 s show the first channel only.
