A separate fork of the StreamLadder-parity prototype, built from what Eklipse users actually do in the editor. Re-checked on 27 October against web editor data from 19,562 projects.

Direction stays “repeat-first”: users repeat a small edit, so make it one click and keep Export in one place. The web data confirms five changes, revises three (rail order, finish bar, what a style contains) and adds two: a review of what AI Edit placed, and a prompt to make 16:9 clips vertical.

Why this direction

Eklipse paid users come back often and change little. A Legacy Premium user who exports on the web averages 11.7 clips in four weeks; a Pro user 13.2. On the web, 72.6% of projects reuse the previous layout.

The tools that go with an exported clip are few: captions, a title, trimming and splitting. Clips that keep the AI output untouched, or stay 16:9, are exported far less often.

So the editor should make the repeated job fast and point users to the few edits that matter. Export stays in one place, the usual style is one click, and the brand sticker is on every clip. Captions and a title are one click away, and the timeline never hides work. When AI Edit has added things, a short review lists them; when a clip is still horizontal, the editor says so.

The full StreamLadder-parity scope decided on 22 October stays. Only order, placement and paths change.

Re-check with web data

The first version (26 Oct) used paid mobile exports because web project contents were not in our database. Thinh's adoption report (web projects created 5-22 Oct, 19,562 projects from 3,695 users, final saved state joined to export) fills that gap. Every gap is an association: people edit more on clips they plan to post.

From evidence to decision

Each row reads left to right: the number, what it tells us about users, the decision it drove in this prototype, and the effect we expect plus the metric that will confirm or refute it.

E1C1C7
Evidence

Web exports per exporting user in 4 weeks: Pro 13.2, Legacy 11.7, Standard 6.2, Free 2.7. Web, 5-22 Oct: first project exported in under 2 min, 79.0% come back within 7 days.

What it shows

Paid users are repeat producers (about 3 clips a week), and a fast first export goes with users coming back.

Decision

Export gets a fixed place (top bar, finish bar, ⌘E) and remembers the last quality.

Impact and what to watch

Every session saves the search for Export and the quality choice. Watch: time from open to export, first-project time to export, 7-day return (52.9% today).

E2C3C6
Evidence

Share of web projects that get exported: Legacy 71%, Pro 69%, Standard 55%, Free 41% (all users 5-22 Oct: 63.5%).

What it shows

29-45% of opened projects end without an export. Standard, the newest paid tier, loses the most (45%).

Decision

Shorten the path to a finished clip: My styles applies the usual edit in one step; the finish bar shows what is done and keeps Export in view.

Impact and what to watch

Expect more opened projects to reach export, mostly for Standard. Watch: project to export rate per tier.

E3C4C3
Evidence

Web: 72.6% of projects reuse the previous layout and export at 76.5% vs 52.8%. Layout preset in 85.7% of projects.

What it shows

Layout is the main edit and most users repeat it from clip to clip.

Decision

Layouts stays first and opens with “Your style” and “Recently used” above the 11-tile gallery.

Impact and what to watch

The usual layout is one click instead of a scroll through 11 tiles. Watch: share of layout picks from Recent/Style.

E4C2C6C5
Evidence

Web, same-user export gaps: text +45.9, trim +39.2, user meme +32.7, split +30.8, auto captions +29.4, background music +14.1 (raw -2.1, 3.8% use).

What it shows

The tools that go with an exported clip are captions, text and timeline edits, not music. Text sits in Elements, last on the old rail.

Decision

Rail: Layouts, Captions, Elements, Effects, Audio. Finish-bar chips: Brand, Layout, Captions, Title. Trim and split stay on the timeline on every tab.

Impact and what to watch

Shorter path to the tools with the biggest gaps. Risk: these are associations; people edit more on clips they plan to post. Watch: tool_used per group against export.

E5C3
Evidence

2,018 templates saved on mobile in 4 weeks; 47,640 by 21,072 users all time. On web, Apply template sits at the bottom of the Export panel. 62.5% of caption projects change the caption style.

What it shows

People want to reuse their setup, including the caption look, but on web reuse is offered only after the work is done.

Decision

My styles in the top bar and as the first card in Layouts; one undo step reverts it; music is optional.

Impact and what to watch

The repeated edit becomes one click at the start of a clip. Watch: style_applied per project, time to export for returning users.

E6C7
Evidence

46% of web-editor exports were posted within 14 days (8 weeks to 5 Oct). StreamLadder lets users post while the clip renders.

What it shows

For about half of exports, posting is the next step. Splitting export and post adds a hand-off where users can drop.

Decision

“Export & post” next to “Export clip” in the export dialog.

Impact and what to watch

Fewer exports that never get posted. Watch: export_and_post use, share rate within 14 days after export.

E7C9
Evidence

Web: AI memes left untouched export 44.4%; AI memes plus one user edit 72.9%. 89.1% of AI-meme projects also get zoom, 61.8% sound effects.

What it shows

Users who accept the AI output as is post far less. Either the AI picks miss, or users who do not engage were not planning to post.

Decision

An AI Edit review strip: keep or remove each AI item, plus one suggested change (title, captions, split).

Impact and what to watch

More AI-edited clips get a user touch and an export. Watch: ai_item_removed by meme, export rate of AI-edited projects, share with at least one user edit.

E8C10
Evidence

Web: 8.2% of projects keep horizontal footage at 16:9 and export at 41.0% vs 66.0% for 9:16. Premium clips: 17.2% stay 16:9.

What it shows

Some clips never become vertical, and those rarely get exported.

Decision

A 16:9 clip shows letterboxed, flags the Layout chip and opens Layouts with “Make it vertical” using the last layout.

Impact and what to watch

Fewer clips left horizontal by accident. Watch: share of 16:9 outputs, export rate of projects that start horizontal.

E9C8C6C3
Evidence

Product input (Thinh, 26/10): the social sticker is always added because it represents the brand. Web: channel-name sticker in 73.1% of projects; 2+ platforms export 79.4% vs 61.8%.

What it shows

The brand sticker is a fixed part of most clips, and a fuller sticker goes with more exports.

Decision

Make it a saved setting: auto-added to every new clip, kept by every style, a Brand chip in the finish bar, its card first in Elements; setup invites more platforms.

Impact and what to watch

No clip leaves without the handle, and nobody re-adds it by hand. Watch: share of exports with a brand sticker, brand_auto turned off, platforms per sticker.

E10
Evidence

Tier differences on mobile are small: Standard has more facecam layers (50.5%), Pro fewer (17.3%) and shorter clips; Pro has only 284 sampled exports. The web data has no tier split.

What it shows

No reliable signal that one tier needs a different editor.

Decision

One editor for every tier; no Basic/Pro mode. Gating stays off for the MVP, as decided on 22 October.

Impact and what to watch

Simpler build and test. Revisit once per-tier tracking exists. Watch: tool_used split by tier.

What changed and why

DATAReason from Eklipse user data   BEST PRACTICEReason from UX best practice

C1

Export and Undo live in one top bar

Kept

Project name, Saved state, Undo/Redo, My styles and Export sit in a fixed top bar on every tab. Export is no longer the sixth rail tab or the last step of a wizard. ⌘E also opens it.

Why · Based on E1

  • DATAPaid users export often: 11.7 web clips per exporting Legacy user and 13.2 per Pro user in 4 weeks (Free: 2.7). The action they repeat most should never move.
  • DATAWeb, 5-22 Oct: users whose first project was exported in under 2 minutes came back within 7 days 79.0% of the time, against 45.0% when the first project was not exported.
  • BEST PRACTICEVideo tools users already know (CapCut, Premiere, Canva, Descript) put Export top right. Users expect the same here. Laws of UX: Jakob's law
  • BEST PRACTICEA large, fixed target in the same corner is faster to hit than a tab whose position depends on the panel. Laws of UX: Fitts's law
C2

Rail ordered by value: Layouts, Captions, Elements, Effects, Audio

Revised 27 Oct

Captions stays second. Elements moves up to third because it holds text, the tool with the largest export gap. Audio goes last. Export leaves the rail.

Why · Based on E4

  • DATAWeb, same user with and without the tool: text +45.9 export points, user-added memes +32.7, auto captions +29.4. Background music is in 3.8% of projects and its raw gap is -2.1.
  • DATAThe first version ordered the rail by how often paid mobile exports contain each thing (captions 9.0%, music 9.3%, stickers 0.4%). On the web, the rarely used text tool turns out to matter most, so frequency alone was the wrong rule.
  • BEST PRACTICEOrder menu items by importance to the task when frequency and value disagree; fewer, better-ordered choices are faster to scan. NN/g: Menu-design checklist
  • BEST PRACTICEEach extra visible choice adds decision time; the rail drops from 6 to 5 items. Laws of UX: Hick's law
C3

My styles: layout and captions in one click, music optional

Revised 27 Oct

A style bundles layout and caption preset, plus music only if the user saved one. Apply it from the top bar or the Layouts panel; one ⌘Z undoes the whole thing. Saved templates appear in the same list, and “Save current edit as a style” sits at the bottom.

Why · Based on E2, E3, E5, E9

  • DATAWeb: 72.6% of projects reuse the layout of the user's previous project, and those export at 76.5% against 52.8% when the layout changes.
  • DATAWeb: 62.5% of projects with captions change the caption style from the most common one, so the caption look is worth saving too. Music is rare (3.8%), so the demo styles no longer add it.
  • DATA2,018 templates were saved on mobile in 4 weeks (47,640 by 21,072 users all time). Today the web Apply button sits at the bottom of the Export panel, after the work is done.
  • BEST PRACTICEAccelerators for frequent users (heuristic 7: flexibility and efficiency of use) without hiding anything from new users. NN/g: 10 usability heuristics
C4

Layouts opens with your style and recent layouts

Kept

The panel starts with a “Your style” card, then “Recently used” (layouts of your last exported clips), then all 11 layouts in a fixed order. Picking a layout never reorders the panel; the selected tile is highlighted wherever it appears.

Why · Based on E3

  • DATAWeb: a layout preset is in 85.7% of projects (+17.4 points for the same user), and 72.6% of projects reuse the previous layout. The usual layout should be the first thing on screen.
  • BEST PRACTICEShow recently used items so people recognize their choice instead of searching the full gallery each time. NN/g: Recognition vs recall
C5

The timeline keeps every lane that has content

Kept

Caption, music, SFX, element and effect lanes stay visible on every tab, Layouts included. Empty lanes collapse. Split and trim stay one click away on every tab.

Why · Based on E4

  • DATAWeb: trim (22.9% of projects, +39.2 points for the same user) and split (8.7%, +30.8) are among the tools that go most with an exported clip. They live on the timeline, so the timeline must stay complete.
  • BEST PRACTICEConsistency and visibility of system status (heuristics 1 and 4): the project should not appear to change when only the tool changes. NN/g: 10 usability heuristics
C6

A finish bar replaces Back / Next: Brand, Layout, Captions, Title

Revised 27 Oct

Four chips show what the clip has, jump to that tool, and turn to a check when done. Title replaces Music: one click adds a title at the top of the clip and opens the text editor. Export clip sits on the right. Nothing is a required step. Below 1400 px the chips show their detail as a tooltip.

Why · Based on E2, E4, E9

  • DATAWeb: text is in only 5.8% of projects but has the largest same-user gap (+45.9). Captions give +29.4 for 1.8 extra minutes, the best value for the time spent. Music has no gain, so it loses its chip.
  • BEST PRACTICEWizards suit rare, linear tasks for novices. Repeat users doing 1-2 edits per clip should not walk through six steps. NN/g: Wizards
  • BEST PRACTICEThe chips also show status at a glance (heuristic 1). NN/g: 10 usability heuristics
C7

Export dialog remembers your quality and offers Export & post

Kept

Export opens as a dialog on any tab. The last quality is pre-selected next time. “Export & post” sits next to “Export clip”. Escape and the X close it.

Why · Based on E1, E6

  • DATA46% of web-editor exports were posted within 14 days. Posting is the usual next step, so it belongs next to Export.
  • DATAStreamLadder opens the publish page while the clip renders, and posts before rendering ends (audit 2026-10-19).
  • BEST PRACTICEGood defaults: repeat users keep the setting they chose last time instead of choosing it again. NN/g: The power of defaults
C8

Brand sticker on every clip: set once, added automatically

Kept

The social sticker (platform icon + handle) is a saved setting. Every new clip starts with it from 0:00 to the end, placed under the facecam and above the captions. Every style keeps it. A “Your brand sticker” card sits at the top of Elements: change the handle once and every sticker updates; the last picked design becomes the brand design; “Add to every new clip” is on by default. The finish bar has a Brand chip.

Why · Based on E9

  • DATAWeb: the channel-name sticker is already in 73.1% of projects. Users keep the usual color in 98.7% and the usual font in 84.7%, so a saved default matches what they do.
  • DATAWeb: 86.8% show one platform, but projects whose sticker lists 2 or more platforms export at 79.4% against 61.8%. The brand setup should invite adding more platforms.
  • BEST PRACTICEGood defaults: something every clip needs should be on by default, not re-added by hand about 12 times a month. NN/g: The power of defaults
  • BEST PRACTICEConsistency: the same handle, design and position on every clip is what makes it a brand. NN/g: Consistency and standards
C9

AI Edit review: keep or remove what the AI added

New 27 Oct

When AI Edit has placed memes, zooms or sounds, a strip under the top bar says what it added. Review opens a list: jump to each item, keep it or remove it (undoable). Under the list, one suggested change: add a title, add captions or split. Keep all closes the strip.

Why · Based on E7

  • DATAWeb: projects with AI memes the user left untouched export 44.4% (3,205 projects). When the user also trimmed, added text, captions or their own meme: 72.9%.
  • DATAWeb: AI-placed memes are in 20.1% of projects with a -4.1 same-user gap, the only tool rated “fix”. 89.1% of AI-meme projects also get the zoom effect and 61.8% get sound effects.
  • DATADeleted AI memes leave no trace in saved projects, so the real editor should log each removal (ai_item_removed) to learn which AI picks users reject.
  • BEST PRACTICEUser control and freedom (heuristic 3): automatic edits need an easy way to inspect and undo them. NN/g: 10 usability heuristics
C10

Make it vertical: a 16:9 clip opens Layouts with your last layout

New 27 Oct

When the output is still 16:9, the phone preview shows the clip letterboxed, the Layout chip turns amber (“16:9, make vertical”), and Layouts opens with a “Make it vertical” card using the user's last layout. Picking any layout also makes the clip 9:16. “Keep 16:9 for YouTube” leaves it as it is.

Why · Based on E8

  • DATAWeb: 8.2% of projects take horizontal footage and stay 16:9; they export at 41.0% against 66.0% for horizontal to 9:16.
  • DATAPremium clips stay 16:9 most often (17.2%), then demo clips (10.0%) and AI clips (8.6%).
  • BEST PRACTICEVisibility of system status (heuristic 1): the output format should be obvious before export, not discovered after. NN/g: 10 usability heuristics

Try the prototype

  1. Open the blank clip. In Layouts, press Apply on “My FPS clips”: layout and captions appear together, and the finish bar shows the checks. Press ⌘Z once to undo all of it.
  2. On the default clip, look at the rail (Layouts, Captions, Elements, Effects, Audio) and the finish bar. Click Title: a title is added at the top and the text editor opens.
  3. Open the AI Edit clip. The strip says what AI Edit added. Press Review, remove one meme (⌘Z brings it back), keep a zoom, then try “Add a title”. Keep all closes the strip.
  4. Open the 16:9 clip. The preview is letterboxed and the Layout chip is amber. Press “Make 9:16”: the clip takes your last layout and the warning goes. ⌘Z returns to 16:9.
  5. Open My styles in the top bar, then “Save current edit as a style”.
  6. Press ⌘E or Export. Pick 720p60, close, reload, open Export again: 720p60 is still selected.
  7. On the blank clip, the Brand chip is already checked. Click Brand, type a new handle in “Your brand sticker”: the sticker and the chip update. Apply a style: the sticker stays.

Verification and limits

  • 27 Oct re-check (headless Chromium, 1600×900 and 1280×800): rail order, Title chip opens the text editor, AI review lists 7 items, remove then undo restores the row, keep removes it from the list, Keep all closes the strip, Make 9:16 clears the warning and undo brings it back. No console errors.
  • The 26 Oct checks (My styles apply and undo, quality remembered after reload, export dialog, tabs) re-run on the new build: all pass, no console errors.
  • TypeScript, colour-token check and Vite build pass.
  • Not verified: real render, real posting, real AI Edit output, cloud save, user testing. Export and AI stay simulated as in the reference prototype.

To measure in the real build: style_applied / style_saved, tool_used per group per project, ai_item_removed, output ratio at export, export_opened by source, export_completed, export_and_post, time from open to export, and 7-day return after the first project.

Sources and open questions

Open questions

  1. Should styles be saved per game/channel (“Valorant”, “Just chatting”) or as one list?
  2. Should Export & post open the Publish page while rendering, like StreamLadder?
  3. Does the web adoption data have a plan field? The design targets paid users, and the web numbers cover everyone.
  4. AI Edit: should we also test fewer AI memes per clip, or turn the zoom and sound bundle off by default, rather than only reviewing it after?
  5. Brand sticker: one per account listing several platforms (proposed from the data). Should Free clips get it on by default too?
