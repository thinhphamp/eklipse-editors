Live word highlight, panel and player in sync

Press play. The line being spoken lights up in the Text tab: words already said get an underline, and the current word gets the purple pill. The same word pops on the video. The list scrolls itself to keep the current line in view. Uses the demo clip and its real word timings (src/fixtures/transcript.json). Speech starts at 0:07.

Highlight

  • Current line: card background + purple bar on the left, purple time.
  • Words already said in that line: purple underline. Current word: purple pill.
  • Player: the same word pops in the caption (pill + slight scale), so panel and video point at the same word.

Auto-scroll

  • When the line changes, the list scrolls smoothly so the current line sits about a third from the top.
  • If you scroll the list yourself while it plays, auto-scroll pauses and "↓ Back to current line" shows. Click it (or any word) to follow again.
  • Seeking on the timeline or with ⏮ ⏭ jumps the list too.

Click and edit

  • Click a word → the video jumps to that word. Click a time → jumps to the line start.
  • Double-click a line or use ✎ to edit it (text selected, Enter saves, Esc cancels). Playback pauses while you type.
  • Change from D1: a single click now seeks instead of editing, like the reference.
